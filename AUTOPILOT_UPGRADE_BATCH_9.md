## Scope boundaries

- Nothing automatic posts stock, money or quality approval, or sends a customer message. Rate cards and enquiries are prepared for a person; scrap sales, weight notes, register confirmations, reservations and drawing proposals are confirmed by a person; scale readings are labelled with their source; the cash forecast is a read model.
- AP-073 cards are priced for every alloy group of active profiles; profile families are listed per alloy because conversion rates are not set per family. Rates exclude GST, which is printed.
- AP-074 sells in-house scrap only. Scrap invoices have their own gap-free series and are not part of the dispatch invoice table (which requires an order and a packed dispatch); they are not yet in the Tally export. Receipts are recorded on the sale.
- AP-075 credit notes post to the invoice; debit notes are numbered documents on the weight-difference record and appear in the dispute register, not as a new receivable.
- AP-076 assumptions: an invoice without a due date is due 30 days after its date; a bill without one is due 30 days after its date; purchase orders are paid 15 days (delivery) plus the vendor's stated credit days (30 when not stated) after ordering, less what was already billed; invoiced scrap is expected 7 days after invoicing. Bills still pending approval are not counted.
- AP-095 covers labels, not database messages; Hindi is Devanagari with shop-floor terms (बिलेट, डाई, पुश, बंडल) kept as operators say them.
- AP-008 reads Hindi/Hinglish only where the item line itself follows the code-length-quantity pattern; free sentences without a code become lines with "No profile code found".
- AP-009 has no image reading (no vision provider is configured; the batch 8 decision on a provider applies here too). The shift window is the press's shift hours from factory capacity when a shift name is given, else the whole day.
- AP-010 Web Serial works in Chrome and Edge on a desktop; phones and other browsers type the weight (labelled typed). A direct gateway call can still claim "scale"; the raw indicator line and the person are recorded.
- AP-048 reads ASCII DXF only (not DWG or binary DXF), ignores blocks (warned), and treats a single rectangle holding a small section as a drawing frame (warned; the preview shows what was used). Densities: alloy master first, else 2700 kg/m³ for 6xxx (6061/6063/6082), 2705 for 1050/1060, 2810 for 7075.

Migration `supabase/migrations/20261116100000_autopilot_batch_9.sql` (additive, one file). New tables (22): `rate_cards`, `user_language_preferences`, `scrap_grades`, `scrap_offers`, `scrap_sales`, `scrap_sale_allocations`, `scrap_sale_receipts`, `scrap_invoice_counters`, `customer_weight_tolerances`, `weight_difference_notes`, `weight_debit_note_counters`, `cash_forecast_settings`, `cash_fixed_expenses`, `cash_one_offs`, `cash_forecast_snapshots`, `quote_enquiries`, `enquiry_code_mappings`, `register_templates`, `register_captures`, `register_capture_rows`, `bundle_weight_captures`, `profile_drawing_reads`. One additive column: `pcda_master_alloys.density_kg_m3` (nullable, 2500–3000). Nine gateways (`ap_rate_card`, `ap_scrap_sale`, `ap_weight_note`, `ap_cash_forecast`, `ap_language`, `ap_enquiry`, `ap_register`, `ap_scale`, `ap_geometry`) take company and actor from `auth.uid()`, refuse dealers and inactive users, check roles per command and pin `search_path`. Batch 3's `ap_cash` (receipts) is untouched — the forecast gateway has its own name (test-enforced). Triggers: `zzz_ap9_stock_arrival` on `profile_stock_batches` (proposals only; errors are caught so it never blocks a stock change) and `ap9_sale_allocation_lot` on `scrap_sale_allocations` (moves a foundry lot's available weight; never below zero). Proposal types `stock.reservation` and `profile.geometry` added by rename-and-wrap of `ap_proposal_versions/editable/apply`; AP-001 registry and executor wrapped with `stock.arrival_reservations` (hourly) and `cash.forecast_snapshot` (nightly). No existing table or column is altered or dropped (the proposal type check is replaced with a superset, as earlier batches did).

Files: register photos and DXF drawings are stored in the company's `documents` folder; the server actions read them back through the person's own storage access, hash them and refuse paths outside the company folder. The stored drawing preview must match the generator's single-path SVG and is displayed as an image. Customer-facing output: the rate card PDF and WhatsApp text carry selling prices, validity and GST only (no billet, conversion, floor, cost or margin — unit-tested); the scrap invoice shows the sale rate and GST. Cash forecast, scrap money commands and weight-note approval are limited to owner, admin and accounts.

Server-side validation: Zod schemas in `lib/autopilot-batch9/commands.ts`, `lib/actions/autopilot-batch9.ts`, `lib/actions/physical-bundles.ts`, `app/api/floor-language` and `app/api/quote-enquiry`; the database re-checks every command. Browser bundles of the floor screens and the quotation form contain no server module (unit-tested). No new dependency.
