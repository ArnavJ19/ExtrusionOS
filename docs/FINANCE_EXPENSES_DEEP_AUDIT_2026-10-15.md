# Finance and Expenses Deep Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-15  
**Scope:** Receivables, invoice posting, customer receipts and reversals, return credit notes, expense ledger, supplier expense payments, finance analytics and exports, invoice/finance PDFs, and profitability summaries.  
**Method:** Read-only source review of the current working tree, including local untracked migration candidates. No application code or existing audit file was changed.

## Evidence Boundary

Findings below are confirmed by source unless explicitly marked conditional. This audit did not connect to a hosted database, inspect production rows or effective grants, replay migrations, run authenticated UI journeys, run tests, reproduce concurrency in separate database sessions, or render PDFs for visual inspection. Therefore this report does not establish deployed behavior or production data impact.

The current working tree contains local, untracked candidate migrations `20261015020000_credit_settlement_exclusivity.sql` and `20261015030000_credit_note_retry_binding.sql`. They are not treated as deployed or release-verified fixes. Historical rows that predate a new guard also need a conflict scan and correction plan.

Severity: **P1** means a direct accounting, approval, or security/integrity risk; **P2** means a material reconciliation, reporting, traceability, or document gap. Conditional findings state the missing proof.

## Findings

### Receivables and Customer Credits

**AR-01 — P1: Standalone credit adjustments and return credit notes can settle the same invoice twice.** The payment RPC accepts `payment_method='credit_note'`, stores it as a paid `ADJUSTMENT`, and includes it in `amount_paid` (`supabase/migrations/20260718010000_financial_closure.sql:533-590`). Return credit notes separately increase `credited_amount`; invoice balance and customer credit are calculated from both values (`supabase/migrations/20260723070000_dispatch_return_credit_notes.sql:3-12,167-190`). For a 100 invoice, a 100 standalone credit adjustment followed by a full return credit can produce 100 `amount_paid`, 100 `credited_amount`, and 100 customer credit due even though no cash was received. The payment UI warning is not a database invariant. The local candidate exclusivity trigger in `20261015020000_credit_settlement_exclusivity.sql` appears intended to prevent new overlaps, but it is untracked and not hosted-verified; scan and resolve historical conflicts as well.

**AR-02 — P2: Customer refunds have no outgoing transaction path.** The UI displays `customer_credit_due` as “Customer refund due” (`components/modules/dispatch-credit-note-client.tsx:62-63`), but the payment action accepts positive inbound receipts and credit adjustments; reversal cancels a receipt rather than recording money sent to the customer (`lib/actions/payments.ts:53-90`; `supabase/migrations/20260718010000_financial_closure.sql:530-550,680-698`). The due amount cannot be cleared or reconciled after an actual cash refund made outside the system. Add an auditable refund/disbursement transaction linked to the invoice and credit note, with balance updates and reporting.

**AR-03 — P2: Payment idempotency keys are not bound to the submitted payment details.** On a replay, the atomic payment function checks that the key belongs to the same invoice, then returns the existing payment as a successful replay without comparing amount, date, method, reference, or notes (`supabase/migrations/20260718010000_financial_closure.sql:455-480`). The UI retains the key while the invoice remains selected (`components/modules/financials/payment-form-client.tsx:51,103-121`). If the original request commits but its response is lost, an operator can edit the amount/details and retry; the system reports success while retaining the old payload. Bind the key to a normalized payload hash and reject changed retries, or require a new key after edits.

**AR-04 — P2: A late partial receipt can clear the invoice's overdue status.** The current invoice status recalculation selects `partially_paid` whenever effective receipts are positive before it checks whether the due date has passed (`supabase/migrations/20260929000000_business_logic_fixes.sql:397-405`). A still-open, overdue invoice therefore becomes `partially_paid` after a receipt or recalculation, making stored status filters disagree with the due-date state. Preserve overdue status when a balance remains past due, while retaining the separate paid/partial amount fields.

**AR-05 — P2, business rule to confirm: Partial return/damage crediting is unsupported.** The only credit action is labeled “Issue full dispatch credit note” (`components/modules/dispatch-credit-note-client.tsx:65`), and the database copies the full original invoice totals and items (`supabase/migrations/20260723070000_dispatch_return_credit_notes.sql:162-190`). If operations can return/damage only part of a dispatch, the current action credits the full invoice. Confirm whole-dispatch-only policy or add returned quantities/amounts and partial credit-note line calculations.

**AR-06 — P2: Customer credit due has no portfolio-level follow-up metric.** The invoice stores `customer_credit_due`, and the dispatch credit panel shows it, but the management analytics snapshot does not include a customer refund/credit obligation KPI or list. Accounts may miss accumulated credits across invoices. Add a company-scoped outstanding customer-credit total and actionable detail list, consistent with the refund/disbursement model in AR-02.

### Expenses and Supplier Payments

**EXP-01 — P1: Positive expense totals can disagree with their components.** The expense schema does not require `total_amount` to equal base plus tax plus freight less discount (`lib/validations/schemas.ts:654-690`). The form exposes all components and total as independent inputs (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:924-930`); its fallback calculation only runs when the submitted total is zero (`components/modules/operations/record-form-client.tsx:763-768`). The database trigger trusts a positive total and derives it only when the total is non-positive (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:159-164`). Example: base 100, tax 18, total 100 persists as a 100 expense and payable. Derive the total in the trusted database path or reject any cent-level mismatch; also reconcile base against quantity times rate where that relationship applies.

**EXP-02 — P1: The ₹25,000 expense approval threshold is bypassable in the normal UI.** The approval trigger intends to hold expenses above ₹25,000 for approval, but its high-value branch only changes submitted `draft` or `pending_approval` states. A submitted `approved` value remains approved (`supabase/migrations/20260531020000_expenses_phase3_energy_maintenance_approval.sql:25-54,199-202`). The form exposes `approval_status='approved'` (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:942`), and an accounts user is allowed to create expenses through the page and RLS (`app/(dashboard)/expenses/new/page.tsx:8-9`; `lib/auth/permissions.ts:141`; `20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:106-116`). Derive approval state from a dedicated authorized approval transition; never trust a client-supplied approved state.

**EXP-03 — P1: Supplier payments are not blocked for unapproved or cancelled expenses.** The supplier payment expense lookup has no approval/payment-state filter (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:999`); validation checks only that an expense ID exists and the amount is positive (`lib/validations/schemas.ts:693-701`). The payment insert policy checks company and role, while the aggregation trigger does not reject draft, pending, rejected, or cancelled parents (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:120-124,191-215`). Accounts can therefore post a payment against an expense that has not been approved or was cancelled/rejected. Enforce eligible parent state in the same database transaction and constrain the selection UI.

**EXP-04 — P1: Concurrent supplier payments can exceed the expense total while the balance projection remains understated.** `sync_expense_payment_totals` sums payment rows before updating the expense and does not lock the parent before that sum (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:191-225`). Two concurrent 60 payments against a 100 expense can each see only their own uncommitted row, then both write a 60 paid projection; the payment ledger can total 120 while `amount_paid` remains 60 and balance remains 40. This is a source-level race analysis, not a two-session reproduction. Lock the parent first, then recompute and enforce the cap in the transaction.

**EXP-05 — P1: Editing a supplier payment to another expense leaves the old expense balance stale.** The payment trigger chooses `coalesce(NEW.expense_ledger_id, OLD.expense_ledger_id)` and recomputes only that parent (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:202-213`). The edit page and expense selector allow the linked expense to change (`app/(dashboard)/expenses/payments/[id]/edit/page.tsx:6-10`; `components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:999`). Reassignment updates the new expense while the old expense retains the payment in its projected totals. On update, recompute both old and new parents in a stable lock order.

**EXP-06 — P1, conditional: Expense payment links are not tenant-composite references.** `expense_payments.company_id` and `expense_ledger_id` are separate; the FK validates only the expense UUID, while insert RLS validates the payment row's company/role (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:80-99,120-124`). A Company A caller who knows a valid Company B expense UUID can create a disconnected Company A payment row; the trigger's company-qualified parent update silently affects no parent. No cross-tenant data read was demonstrated. Add a composite `(company_id, expense_ledger_id)` reference or reject mismatched parent ownership in a transactional trigger/RPC.

**EXP-07 — P1, conditional on effective table grants: Expense write RLS is broader than the finance page permission.** The `expense_ledger` insert/update policies include production, dispatch, inventory, and factory roles alongside finance roles (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:106-116`), while the normal expenses pages require `can(role, ..., 'financials')`. If authenticated PostgREST table DML grants are present, these roles can write expense rows directly despite the page restriction. Effective hosted grants were not inspected. Align RLS roles with intended finance authority or prove the lower-level grants deny direct writes.

**EXP-08 — P2: Expense payment entries are non-idempotent and payment references are not unique.** `reference_number` is nullable and only indexed non-uniquely (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:80-99`); the form permits a blank reference (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:1002-1004`). Repeated submissions can create duplicate supplier payments if the operator retries. Source-linked duplicate vendor invoice detection only raises a warning and continues; there is no unique vendor/invoice invariant (`20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:311-323`). Add an idempotency key for payment posting and a duplicate bill control that accounts for legitimate vendor numbering practices.

**EXP-09 — P2: Supplier payment history is mutable and audit entries omit before-values.** RLS permits expense-payment updates (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:125-130`), and an edit route exposes the payment form. The update audit records only the new expense ID, amount, and date, not the old/new reference or method (`20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:255-258`). This allows original payment evidence to be overwritten without a complete audit trail. Prefer reversal/adjustment rows with original transaction linkage and record before/after values.

**EXP-10 — P2: Source-level paid status and the central expense payment ledger can disagree.** `upsert_source_expense` passes a source record's `payment_status` into the central expense, but `expense_apply_totals` derives status from central `amount_paid`; source sync does not create an `expense_payments` row (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:146-184,271-376`). A source marked paid can appear unpaid in Expenses, and a later central payment does not necessarily update the source display. Define one authoritative payment ledger and synchronize both projections transactionally.

**EXP-11 — P2: Soft-deleting a paid source removes spend from metrics while retaining payment rows.** Source deletion handlers cancel/soft-delete the central expense (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:554-561,692-699,816-823`), but do not void related `expense_payments`; management metrics then exclude `deleted_at` expenses (`20261008040000_management_metrics_integrity.sql:69-72`). Cash paid can disappear from spend KPIs while payment history remains. Use an auditable reversal/void path and keep paid historical spend represented.

**EXP-12 — P2: Supplier credit notes/refunds cannot reduce the payable ledger.** Expense totals and supplier payment entries are modeled as positive amounts, and `expensePaymentSchema` requires a positive payment (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:55-64,95-96`; `lib/validations/schemas.ts:693-701`). No supplier-credit-note or outgoing-refund action was found. A vendor credit cannot be reconciled against an original bill; it must be handled outside the module. Add a linked credit/adjustment transaction with explicit effects on expense and payable balances.

### Access Control

**SEC-01 — P2, conditional on intended role visibility and effective SELECT grants: Sales managers can read invoice headers through PostgREST despite the app route denying finance access.** The collection analytics migration recreates `invoices_finance_read` with `sales_manager` included (`supabase/migrations/20260718010100_financial_collection_analytics.sql:3-8`). The role matrix does not grant that role `financials` read permission, and `/invoices` redirects it (`lib/auth/permissions.ts:21,76-82,138-145`; `app/(dashboard)/invoices/page.tsx:6-9`). A direct authenticated table query may therefore expose company-scoped invoice header totals, status, due dates, notes, and party snapshots while bypassing the route gate. Invoice line policies remain finance-only, and no cross-company access was identified. Confirm whether this role should see invoice headers; if not, remove it from RLS and serve only the approved aggregate analytics.

### Analytics, Reports, and PDFs

**RPT-01 — P1/P2: Management expense metrics include unapproved and void states.** The expense CTE filters by company and `deleted_at`, but not approval or payment state (`supabase/migrations/20261008040000_management_metrics_integrity.sql:69-72`); gross and paid aggregates then include those rows (`:101-102,117`). Draft, pending, rejected, cancelled, and reversed expenses can inflate spend/run-rate and distort paid-rate metrics when they remain undeleted. Define booked spend and commitments separately and filter each metric accordingly.

**RPT-02 — P2: Invoice analytics is gross invoicing and does not net return credits.** MTD/monthly invoice metrics sum the original `grand_total`, including invoices whose status is `credited`, and do not include `credited_amount` or credit-note rows (`supabase/migrations/20261008040000_management_metrics_integrity.sql:65-68,97,116`). This can be a valid gross-billed metric, but it cannot reconcile to receivables/net billed value. Label it gross and report credit-note value and net billings alongside it.

**RPT-03 — P1: Payment exports can report cancelled receipts as positive collections.** Report Builder's payment source exposes date, method, amount, invoice, and created date but not status/type (`components/modules/report-builder-client.tsx:30-33`); its query selects all matching tenant rows without a posted-status filter (`:232-248`). Reversing a receipt retains its row as `CANCELLED` (`supabase/migrations/20260718010000_financial_closure.sql:680-698`), while authoritative collection analytics includes only `PAID` and signs refunds negative (`20260718010100_financial_collection_analytics.sql:30-40`). The export therefore counts a cancelled receipt as positive and cannot sign refund rows. Add status/type fields and apply the same effective-collection rules as the authoritative query.

**RPT-04 — P2: Expense exports cannot be reconciled for currency or deleted records.** Report Builder's expense field list omits currency and deleted state, and its generic query applies no `deleted_at` exclusion (`components/modules/report-builder-client.tsx:25-33,232-248`). A saved export can therefore contain soft-deleted expense rows without identifying their currency. Include currency and deletion/status fields and use the same filtering rules as the management metrics.

**RPT-05 — P2: Profitability cards silently calculate from only the latest 500 records.** The profitability page limits fetched rows to 500 before computing Revenue, Total Cost, and Margin summaries (`app/(dashboard)/profitability/page.tsx:44-46,60-78,104-109`). Once the company exceeds that history, the cards understate totals without showing the coverage boundary. Use server-side aggregates or make the date/row scope explicit in the labels.

**RPT-06 — P2: Invoice detail cannot reconcile paid and credited amounts to its displayed balance.** The receivable section omits `amount_paid` and `credited_amount`, and only links Customer Receipts, not the invoice's credit notes (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:1119-1122,1137-1139`). A return credit can change balance without a visible transaction explaining it. Show invoice total, cash received, credit notes, balance, and linked history together.

**RPT-07 — P2: Generic currency display rounds to whole rupees and labels every currency as INR.** The expense schema/form accepts INR, USD, EUR, AED, GBP, and other (`lib/validations/schemas.ts:672`; `components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:930`), but the generic value renderer uses `₹` and zero decimal places (`components/modules/operations/value.tsx:16`; `lib/utils/format.ts:8-10`). Expense analytics also drops currency and sums nominal values together (`20261008040000_management_metrics_integrity.sql:69-72,101-102`). Non-INR amounts are mislabeled and mixed into INR totals; paise-level differences are hidden in the UI. Restrict entry to INR until conversion is implemented, or store/display source currency plus a governed base-currency rate and retain appropriate precision.

**PDF-01 — P2: Invoice PDFs remove non-ASCII legal/customer text.** The PDF text helper strips characters outside printable ASCII (`lib/pdf/invoice-pdf.ts:22-24,43-59`). Indian-language names/addresses can disappear or become unreadable. Embed a suitable Unicode font and preserve source text.

**PDF-02 — P2: Invoice continuation pages lose invoice identity and page context.** Page breaks add a new page and reset vertical position, but the invoice header/party details are only rendered before the item loop (`lib/pdf/invoice-pdf.ts:37-54`). Later item/totals pages have no repeated invoice number/header or page numbering. Repeat a compact identity header and add page numbers.

**PDF-03 — P2: Invoice PDFs omit due-date/payment context and can overflow on long unbroken text.** The PDF API does not select `due_date`, paid amount, or balance (`app/api/pdf/invoice/[id]/route.ts:14-16`), and the renderer prints invoice number and issue date but no due-date/receivable context (`lib/pdf/invoice-pdf.ts:43-52`). The line wrapper only splits on whitespace; a long profile identifier or description without spaces can exceed the printable width (`invoice-pdf.ts:28-33`). Include the required payment terms/balance context and hard-wrap oversized tokens.

**PDF-04 — P2: Finance report PDFs are not reconciled statements.** Report Builder's PDF export prints group labels and rows but no per-group or grand totals (`components/modules/report-builder-client.tsx:373-388,422-468`); it also has no `credit_notes` source (`:25-43`) and truncates PDF output to the first six selected fields (`:378,470-472`). A selected amount/status field beyond column six is omitted, and the output cannot reconcile invoice, receipt, refund, and credit-note totals. Add explicit statement totals, complete financial fields, and credit-note source support.

**PDF-05 — P2 capability gap: No credit-note, expense voucher, or supplier-payment PDF was found.** The credit-note panel shows note metadata/actions but no document action (`components/modules/dispatch-credit-note-client.tsx:62-67`); the route/source inventory has an invoice PDF but no credit-note, expense, or supplier-payment voucher builder/route. This audit could not assess their visual readability because they are not implemented. Add printable documents if these transactions must be shared or retained as vouchers.

## Safeguards Confirmed in Source

The financial closure migration drops prior invoice/payment policies and recreates SELECT-only policies; invoice items are also read-only to clients (`supabase/migrations/20260718010000_financial_closure.sql:733-804`). - Return credit notes have an atomic issue/reversal path and preserve item snapshots; the identified gap is settlement overlap, reconciliation/reporting, retry binding, and missing printable documents, not absence of the credit-note records themselves.
- The invoice write path pins company/order/dispatch lineage and restricts edits to eligible unpaid draft/generated invoices (`supabase/migrations/20260929000000_business_logic_fixes.sql:814-901`). Invoice packing-line parity, full packing-row coverage, and source-tax canonicalization have database guards (`20260718052000_invoice_packing_line_parity.sql:40-85,96-157`; `20260929000000_business_logic_fixes.sql:1148-1185`). No arithmetic defect was found in that reviewed invoice line/tax calculation path.

## Recommended Order

1. Close settlement integrity first: apply/replay and verify credit settlement exclusivity, scan historical adjustment/credit-note conflicts, bind payment and credit-note retries to payloads, and add an auditable refund/credit transaction path.
2. Fix expense posting invariants: server-derived totals, approval transition enforcement, eligible-parent checks, tenant-composite payment links, locking/cap enforcement, and recomputation of both parent expenses on payment reassignment.
3. Reconcile reporting and history: filter booked spend correctly, separate gross/net invoicing, make exports use the canonical transaction semantics, preserve paid source history, and expose currency/status/credit-note detail.
4. Complete document handling: Unicode-safe invoice rendering, repeated page identity, financial statement totals, and printable credit-note/expense/payment vouchers.

## Second-Pass Additions (2026-10-15)

This pass rechecked the transaction paths and approval/edit controls against the current source and added only findings not already listed above. **P3** denotes a lower-severity workflow or discoverability defect.

**EXP-13 — P1, effective authenticated table grant not verified: `amount_paid` can be changed without an expense payment row.** The expense table's insert/update RLS policies allow finance and several operations roles (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:106-116`). The before-update trigger accepts `NEW.amount_paid` so long as it does not exceed `total_amount`, then derives balance and marks the expense paid when the balance is zero (`:146-184`). It does not compare that value with `sum(expense_payments.amount_paid)`. The approved-expense guard also omits `amount_paid` from its protected columns (`supabase/migrations/20260531010000_expenses_phase2_packaging_and_controls.sql:384-425`). The normal form schema does not expose this field, but an authenticated PostgREST update can forge the projection if the hosted table grant permits it. Make `amount_paid` database-owned from the payment ledger and deny direct column writes or use a tightly scoped posting RPC.

**EXP-14 — P1: An approved expense can be unlocked and edited in two steps.** The client blocks saving only while the submitted form still says `approved` (`components/modules/operations/record-form-client.tsx:661-664`). The database guard blocks changes to selected financial fields only when the old row is approved, and does not make `approval_status` immutable (`supabase/migrations/20260531010000_expenses_phase2_packaging_and_controls.sql:384-425`). An authorized editor can first change the status without changing the protected financial values, then edit the amount/vendor/details after the old status is no longer approved. Remove status changes from ordinary edit and enforce an authorized transition/immutable approved snapshot in the database.

**AR-07 — P2, local candidate migration not release-verified: Credit-note idempotency keys are not bound to dispatch or reason.** The current base function looks up an existing credit note by company and key and returns its ID before validating the requested dispatch/reason (`supabase/migrations/20260723070000_dispatch_return_credit_notes.sql:117-144`). Reusing the key for different details can therefore appear successful while returning a note for the earlier request. `20261015030000_credit_note_retry_binding.sql` is a local untracked candidate that appears to bind retries, but it has not been tracked, replayed, or verified in a hosted database. Reject retries whose normalized dispatch/reason differ and verify the candidate migration through ordered replay.

**UX-01 — P3: Invoice creation is hidden from the invoice module.** Invoice creation uses the dedicated atomic form, but the generic invoice config has no `fields`/`schema`, so the overview and database page do not show their normal create action (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:1132-1136`; `module-overview-client.tsx:111-115`; `module-database-client.tsx:88-90`). The `/invoices/new` route exists, but the only visible link found is from the empty payment form (`components/modules/financials/payment-form-client.tsx:155`). Add a visible link to the dedicated invoice form from the invoice module and dispatch workflow without re-enabling generic client-owned totals.

### Receivables and Invoice Lifecycle

**AR-08 — P2: Open invoices beyond the first 500 cannot be selected for receipt posting.** The payment form fetches open invoices ordered by due date with a hard limit of 500, then builds its only selector options from those rows (`components/modules/financials/payment-form-client.tsx:67-81,97-101`). The `invoice_id` deep link also only selects a row in that truncated result, and submit remains disabled when the selected invoice is absent (`app/(dashboard)/payments/new/page.tsx:6-15`; `payment-form-client.tsx:196`). With more than 500 open invoices, a later invoice cannot be paid through this form. Use server-side search/pagination and resolve a requested invoice ID independently of the first page.

**AR-09 — P1, conditional on legacy duplicates: The active-invoice uniqueness migration can continue without installing its database guard.** `20260718010000_financial_closure.sql` creates the unique company/dispatch index only when no active duplicate dispatch invoices exist; otherwise it emits a warning and skips the index (`:29-39`). The current invoice RPC checks for an existing active invoice but does not lock the dispatch or otherwise serialize that check with insertion (`supabase/migrations/20260929000000_business_logic_fixes.sql:850-871`). Its edit branch also permits changing `order_id` and `dispatch_id` without checking whether the replacement dispatch already has an active invoice (`:912-917,941-947`). If the migration skipped the index, a stale edit form can therefore create a duplicate alongside the original invoice, in addition to concurrent invoice creations passing the unlocked check. Resolve legacy duplicates and fail closed until the unique index is installed; hosted index state and historical duplicate rows were not inspected.

**AR-10 — P2, conditional on timezone settings: Receipt dates use different calendar sources in the UI and database.** The payment form defaults and caps the date using `todayIso()`, which reads the browser's local calendar (`components/modules/financials/payment-form-client.tsx:54,169`; `lib/utils/format.ts:38-43`). The database defaults omitted dates and rejects future dates using session `current_date` (`supabase/migrations/20260718010000_financial_closure.sql:437,536`) without setting the business timezone in that function. If the browser/company timezone and database session timezone differ, a valid business-day date may be rejected or an omitted date may be stored as the prior/next day. For example, with an IST operator and a UTC database session, the form's date can be ahead of `current_date` during the first 5.5 hours of the IST day. Derive and validate both dates using one explicit company business timezone.

**AR-11 — P2: Invoice creation cannot select source orders or dispatches beyond the first 500.** The invoice form fetches only the first 500 eligible orders and dispatches (`components/modules/financials/invoice-form-client.tsx:100-114`), then constructs its selectors from those in-memory results (`:142-150,181-191`). There is no server-side search/pagination or independent lookup for an older source. Once a company has more than 500 eligible rows in either ordered result, an otherwise billable order/dispatch outside the window cannot be selected to create its invoice. Use server-side search/pagination and resolve the selected order and dispatch by ID.

### Access Control

**Additional evidence for SEC-01:** The same role-scope concern also appears in a `SECURITY DEFINER` analytics RPC. `get_advanced_analytics_snapshot` explicitly permits `sales_manager` and returns receivable/collection/expense KPIs plus an overdue invoice list containing invoice number, customer, due date, balance, and status (`supabase/migrations/20261008040000_management_metrics_integrity.sql:13-33,138-157,170-171`). The RPC is executable by authenticated users, while the invoice page still denies the sales-manager role. Confirm whether this customer-level receivable detail is intended for that role; otherwise remove it from this RPC or return an approved aggregate.

**SEC-02 — P2, conditional on authenticated table SELECT grants: Non-finance users can read company expense and supplier-payment rows through PostgREST.** The `expense_ledger` and `expense_payments` SELECT policies check company ownership but not role (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:104-105,118-119`). The Expenses and payment pages require financial read permission (`app/(dashboard)/expenses/page.tsx:8`; `app/(dashboard)/expenses/payments/page.tsx:8`), but those route checks do not constrain direct table queries. If authenticated SELECT grants are effective, same-company non-finance users can read vendor/bill details, totals, balances, payment methods, references, and notes. This is separate from EXP-07's write-policy issue; effective hosted grants were not inspected, and no cross-company read was found.

**SEC-03 — P2, conditional on receipt-level details being finance-only: Sales managers can call the collection-events RPC to inspect individual receipt dates and amounts.** The `SECURITY DEFINER` helper allows `sales_manager` and returns one event UUID, payment date, and signed amount for each paid non-credit-note payment (`supabase/migrations/20260718010100_financial_collection_analytics.sql:10-40`). Its public wrapper is executable by every authenticated user and delegates to that helper (`:44-53`); the default sales-manager role has `export_reports` but not `manage_payments` (`lib/auth/permissions.ts:21,30`), while direct payments-table SELECT is limited to owner, admin, and accounts (`supabase/migrations/20260718010000_financial_closure.sql:778-782`). The RPC omits invoice/customer identifiers, references, and notes, but still exposes receipt-level timing and amounts despite the narrower table-read policy. Confirm whether those event details are intended for sales managers; if access should be aggregate-only, restrict the helper to finance roles or return daily aggregates without event IDs.

### Expenses and Supplier Payments

**EXP-15 — P1: A zero-rate energy edit leaves the previous positive expense in the ledger.** The energy UI accepts a non-negative rate and writes both the new rate and recalculated total cost (`app/(dashboard)/energy/page.tsx:232-254`). When the new total is zero, `sync_energy_reading_expense` returns before calling the source-expense upsert or updating the linked expense (`supabase/migrations/20260531020000_expenses_phase3_energy_maintenance_approval.sql:59-89`). The energy record can therefore show zero cost while the expense ledger retains the old payable and spend amount. Update or reverse the linked expense when a source cost becomes zero.

**EXP-16 — P2: Expense overdue status does not advance when the due date passes.** `expense_apply_totals` sets `payment_status='overdue'` only during an expense insert/update (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:146-184,907-910`). The Expenses database filters on the stored `payment_status` field (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:852`); no date-driven refresh path was found in the repository. An unpaid row saved before its due date can remain `unpaid` and be missing from the Overdue filter until another write happens. Compute overdue status at read time or add a reliable scheduled refresh.

**EXP-17 — P2: Expense paid dates do not reconcile to payment dates and can remain stale after an edit.** When the payment projection reaches the total, `expense_apply_totals` stamps `paid_date` with database `current_date` rather than the settlement payment's `max(payment_date)` (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:173-184,191-225`). The payment aggregation sums amounts only; if a supplier payment is edited down so the expense becomes partial or unpaid, the trigger does not clear the former `paid_date`. Backdated payments can show the wrong settlement date, and reopened balances can still show as paid on that date. Derive `paid_date` from the active payment ledger and clear it when no longer fully settled.

**EXP-18 — P3, if the Paid overview group is intended to reflect settlement: The Paid lane is keyed to approval status, not the payment ledger.** Expenses overview groups by `approval_status` and includes a `paid` group (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:846-848`), but payment aggregation updates `amount_paid` and `payment_status` without changing `approval_status` (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:173-184,202-213`). Fully paid expenses can remain in the Approved lane and fail to appear under Paid. The generic form also exposes `approval_status='paid'`, while the approval trigger accepts that state unchanged (`module-config.ts:942`; `20260531020000_expenses_phase3_energy_maintenance_approval.sql:35-37`), so an approval label can say paid without a matching payment. Use the ledger-derived payment status for the Paid group and do not allow users to set settlement state manually.

**EXP-19 — P1: Foundry external-source and scrap inserts/edits recursively retrigger until the transaction fails.** Their `AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE OR DELETE` triggers call `sync_expense_link_for_sources` (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:883-895,937-945`). On each non-delete call, `sync_external_source_inventory_and_expense` updates the same external-source row (`:619-624,653-657`) and `sync_scrap_inventory_and_expense` updates the same scrap row (`:757-761,791-795`). Those self-updates fire the same unrestricted update triggers again, and these two functions have no `pg_trigger_depth()` guard; unlike the packaging and maintenance sync paths, repeated recursion does not return early. PostgreSQL eventually aborts the transaction with a stack-depth error, rolling back the originating source write and its linked inventory/expense changes. Return the computed fields in a `BEFORE` trigger or add a correct nested-trigger guard and idempotent projection update.

**EXP-20 — P2, conditional on `cancelled` meaning the purchase is void: Cancelling an outsourced billet batch leaves its payable active.** The adapter sets the effective inventory quantity to zero when the source status is `cancelled`, but still calculates the full purchase total and upserts the expense using the source payment status (`supabase/migrations/20260531010000_expenses_phase2_packaging_and_controls.sql:302-303,330-360`). It does not cancel or soft-delete the linked expense. The standard form exposes `cancelled` as a batch status (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:479,521`). A voided batch can therefore disappear from stock while remaining in expense and payable totals. Synchronize the expense cancellation/reversal state with the source status; confirm how cancellation fees should be represented.

**EXP-21 — P2: Maintenance supplier and bill details entered in the UI are omitted from the central expense.** The maintenance form writes free-text `vendor_name` and `bill_number` (`app/(dashboard)/maintenance/page.tsx:320-326`), while the expense sync passes `new.vendor_id` and `new.invoice_number` to `upsert_source_expense` (`supabase/migrations/20260531020000_expenses_phase3_energy_maintenance_approval.sql:173,185`). The maintenance UI does not populate those linked fields, so the central payable loses the vendor snapshot and invoice number; the maintenance screen retains details that the expense ledger and duplicate-vendor-bill check cannot use. Map the entered supplier and bill data into the canonical expense record or use linked vendor/bill controls in the source form.

**EXP-22 — P2, when cost changes without a quantity change: Packaging and outsourced-billet inventory valuations remain stale.** Both adapters calculate `v_delta` from quantity and update `inventory_items.average_rate` and insert a stock movement only inside `if v_delta <> 0` (`supabase/migrations/20260531010000_expenses_phase2_packaging_and_controls.sql:157-184,302-326`). Their expense upserts still run when rate, tax, freight, or total cost changes at the same quantity (`:191-214,330-360`). The expense can reflect the corrected purchase while inventory's weighted rate and movement ledger retain the old cost. Revalue stock and record an adjustment when purchase costs change independently of quantity.

**EXP-23 — P2, conditional on non-INR expense rows: Management expense totals combine currencies without conversion or grouping.** The expense schema and form accept INR, USD, EUR, AED, GBP, and `other` (`supabase/migrations/20260531000000_expenses_module.sql:25,64`; `lib/validations/schemas.ts:672`). The management analytics CTE selects only `total_amount` and `amount_paid`, omits `currency`, then sums those raw amounts into company expense and paid totals (`supabase/migrations/20261008040000_management_metrics_integrity.sql:69-72,101-102,117`). If non-INR values are recorded, the resulting KPIs add unlike currencies as though they shared one unit. Group by currency or normalize using an explicit, dated exchange-rate basis before presenting a consolidated total.

**EXP-24 — P2, when an outsourced billet's alloy or diameter changes: Stock remains on the old inventory item.** The adapter derives the SKU from the new alloy and diameter, then computes inventory delta from quantity/status only (`supabase/migrations/20260531010000_expenses_phase2_packaging_and_controls.sql:296-303`). It applies that delta to the new item and overwrites the source's `inventory_item_id`, without subtracting the old batch quantity from the former item (`:304-326,341-343`). Because alloy and diameter are editable in the standard source form (`components/modules/operations/module-config.ts:497,505`), an edit at unchanged weight leaves the full stock on the old SKU and none on the new one. Reverse the old item quantity and post the new item quantity when SKU-defining fields change, or make those fields immutable after receipt.

**EXP-25 — P2, conditional on authenticated breakdown-row updates being usable: Setting a maintenance breakdown cost to zero leaves its previous central expense active.** The `AFTER INSERT OR UPDATE` adapter returns immediately when `new.cost <= 0`, without changing the linked expense; changing an existing positive-cost row to zero can leave the old payable and spend amount in the ledger (`supabase/migrations/20260531020000_expenses_phase3_energy_maintenance_approval.sql:134-162,210-212`). The maintenance page accepts zero cost (`app/(dashboard)/maintenance/page.tsx:317,671`), and an RLS update policy permits owner, admin, and production-manager roles to update breakdown rows (`supabase/migrations/20260715010000_role_aware_sensitive_rls.sql:314-320`). The page has no breakdown-edit action, so practical reachability depends on authenticated direct-table update grants or another update path; hosted grants were not inspected. When a linked source cost becomes zero, reverse or update its expense instead of leaving the old positive payable active.

### Profitability and Reports

**RPT-08 — P2, conditional on NULL cost components: Profitability calculations can silently turn unknown costs into zero.** Cost components in `order_cost_breakdown` have defaults but are nullable, and the generated `total_cost`/`gross_margin` use ordinary SQL addition, which produces NULL when any component is NULL (`supabase/migrations/0021_enterprise_phase_14.sql:28-49`). The profitability dashboard recomputes those totals in JavaScript, where a NULL component is coerced to zero (`app/(dashboard)/profitability/data.ts:40-47`; `app/(dashboard)/profitability/page.tsx:60-72`). For example, with revenue 200, material cost 100, and conversion cost NULL, the stored generated total and margin are NULL while the dashboard reports cost 100 and margin 100. Make required cost components non-null or mark incomplete costs as unknown; actual NULL-row prevalence was not inspected.

**RPT-09 — P2: Profitability “Avg Pay Days” is not connected to receipt events and includes unpaid rows.** `payment_days` defaults to zero and is averaged across all loaded cost-breakdown rows without a payment-status filter (`supabase/migrations/0021_enterprise_phase_14.sql:51-53`; `app/(dashboard)/profitability/page.tsx:75,111`; `app/(dashboard)/profitability/data.ts:104`). Repository search found no payment/invoice workflow that updates this field. Unpaid or pending rows therefore contribute the default zero, and posted receipts do not establish the displayed payment duration. Derive the metric from canonical receipt events for settled invoices and report outstanding age separately.

**RPT-10 — P3: The PCDA profitability report labels payment state as approval state.** The profitability builder assigns `breakdown.payment_status` to the shared `approvalStatus` header field (`lib/reports/pcda/builder.ts:1181-1192`). The generator prints that field as `Approval:` and the CSV export labels it `Approval Status` (`lib/reports/pcda/generator.ts:15-26,70-74`), while the same template also contains a `Payment Status` field (`builder.ts:1223`). A value such as `paid` is therefore misrepresented as approval metadata. Set a real approval state or omit/rename the generic header for this template.

### PDF Coverage

**PDF-06 — P2: Quote PDFs also remove Indian-script legal and customer text.** The quote PDF sanitizer strips every character above U+00FF (`lib/pdf/quote-pdf.ts:19-29`), and the quote PDF route passes company/customer data to that builder (`app/api/pdf/quote/[id]/route.ts:7-46`; `lib/pdf/quote-pdf.ts:194-206`). Devanagari and other Indian-script names/addresses can disappear from quotations, the same defect class as PDF-01. Add Unicode-capable font support to both invoice and quote renderers.

**Unconfirmed PDF check:** The PCDA report generator uses built-in Helvetica and the builder includes customer names (`lib/reports/pcda/generator.ts:44-60`; `lib/reports/pcda/builder.ts:1198`). No Hindi/Unicode PCDA PDF was rendered in this pass, so whether unsupported characters fail generation or render incorrectly remains unconfirmed and should be checked with representative output.

**PDF-07 — P2, conditional on a drawn value exceeding 110 characters: PCDA PDFs silently truncate long fields.** The generator passes every text value through `text.slice(0, 110)` before drawing and does not wrap the discarded suffix or mark the omission (`lib/reports/pcda/generator.ts:58-67,85-87`). The Profitability Intelligence template includes customer names in the report header/reference and fields (`lib/reports/pcda/builder.ts:1181-1198`), and the source column is unbounded `TEXT` (`supabase/migrations/0021_enterprise_phase_14.sql:12`). Long customer names or references can therefore be cut off in a financial PDF without indicating that information is missing. Wrap to the available page width or visibly mark omitted text.
