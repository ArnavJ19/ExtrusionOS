## Defect found and fixed

**Date time bomb in the batch 6 financial workflow fixture (EOS 97/27).** `scripts/lib/financial-workflow-review-fixture.mjs` dated its invoices relative to today (`current_date-20`, `current_date-10`, `current_date-2`). The tests post receipts on fixed dates (`2026-10-20`, `2026-10-22`).

From about 31 October, INV-1102's date moved past those receipt dates. The accounting-period guard (`20261021190000`) then correctly rejected the receipts with "A payment receipt … cannot predate its invoice". Five receipt tests failed.

The fix pins the invoice dates to fixed values (`2026-10-01`, `2026-10-05` and `2026-10-10`). Due dates stay relative, so INV-1101 is still overdue and INV-1102 is still current.

This was a test defect only. The application logic and the guard behave correctly. The other fixtures that mix relative and fixed dates were checked, and none of them has the same predate dependency.

## Static audit of batch 6 (migrations `20261029110000`–`20261029150000`)

- **RLS:** all 25 new `public` tables enable RLS and carry `company_id`.
- **Private tables:** the three `private.*_authorizations` tables revoke all access from `public`, `anon` and `authenticated`.
- **Security-definer functions:** every one pins `search_path`.
- **Anonymous access:** no grants to `anon`.
- **Public RPCs:** every one checks the actor role and company. The `save_reviewed_invoice_atomic` wrapper delegates to a private function that enforces the `owner`/`admin`/`accounts` roles.
- **`extensions.digest`:** `20261029150000` calls `extensions.digest`, which is correct for hosted Supabase, where pgcrypto lives in `extensions`.
- **Customer portal (EOS 52):** `private.portal_order_projection` returns only these fields:
  - order header, stage and dates
  - delivery address
  - line profile and quantities
  - dispatch number, date, status and weight

  It returns no price, cost, margin or internal notes. The portal is scoped to `portal_users.customer_id`, and responses are version-bound and idempotent.
- **Purchasing database page:** paginates server-side, 10 rows per page, with search, filters and sorting.
- **Placeholder UI:** no "coming soon", TODO or FIXME markers in `app`, `components` or `lib`.
